Dinero is a Danish accounting system. Because TimeWren is self-hosted, you register your own OAuth app at Visma Connect and give the credentials to the server. After that, every user connects their own Dinero account with one click.

> **Note:** API access requires a Dinero Pro organization. Without one, connecting fails with "No Dinero Pro organisation found".

## What it does

- Imports Dinero contacts as clients (already imported contacts are skipped).
- Exports a client to Dinero.
- Exports an invoice as a draft, booked, or booked and sent. You choose the revenue account, payment terms and one line per entry or a combined line.
- Syncs payment status back, so paid invoices are marked as paid in TimeWren.

## Sending by email

When you choose to book and send the invoice, TimeWren first shows a confirmation with the recipient, invoice number, amount including VAT, line count and due date as TimeWren calculates them. Dinero calculates the final amount with its own VAT settings. If the invoice changes after you open the confirmation, TimeWren refuses to send it and you open the confirmation again.

The same invoice is never exported twice at once. If you click twice, or retry while the export is running, TimeWren refuses the second attempt, so Dinero neither creates nor emails the invoice twice.

If Dinero does not answer while the email is being sent, or TimeWren is interrupted in the middle of it, TimeWren cannot tell whether the client received the invoice. The invoice page then shows a warning, and the invoice cannot be sent again from TimeWren until you have checked the email status in Dinero and marked whether it was sent.

If you delete the invoice in Dinero, automatic status sync releases the link in TimeWren, but only when the connected Dinero organization is provably the one the invoice was exported to: TimeWren recorded the organization at the export, or, for an invoice exported before that, the client has not changed since the day before the export and the client's contact still exists in the connected organization. Otherwise TimeWren keeps the link. A draft without a link can be exported again.

## Server setup

1. Register an application at Visma Connect (the developer portal for Dinero's public API) and set this redirect URI, exactly as written:

   ```text
   https://timewren.example.com/api/integrations/dinero/callback
   ```

2. Add the credentials to `.env`:

   ```dotenv
   DINERO_CLIENT_ID=your-client-id
   DINERO_CLIENT_SECRET=your-client-secret
   # Only if the callback differs from NEXT_PUBLIC_APP_URL + /api/integrations/dinero/callback
   # DINERO_REDIRECT_URI=https://timewren.example.com/api/integrations/dinero/callback
   ```

3. Restart the app.

The sign-in uses the scopes `openid`, `dineropublicapi:read`, `dineropublicapi:write` and `offline_access`. The flow posts back to your callback URL from another site, so your instance must run on HTTPS. Local plain-HTTP testing needs an HTTPS tunnel.

`ENCRYPTION_KEY` must be set, because the OAuth tokens are stored encrypted. See [Environment variables](/docs/configuration/environment).

Without the client id, the Connect button redirects back with a `dinero_not_configured` error.

## Connect as a user

1. Open Settings, Integrations and choose Connect to Dinero.
2. Sign in at Visma Connect and approve access. If you have access to several Dinero organizations, choose which one to connect.
3. Choose the default revenue account (required for invoice export). Optionally map Dinero products for hours and for prepaid packages.
4. Under Default accounting system, optionally turn on Automatic status sync.

Contacts are imported from the Sync page under Integrations. Invoices are exported from the invoice itself.

Older connections made with a Dinero API key keep working. The settings page offers an upgrade to OAuth.

## Sync and webhooks

The `cron` container calls `/api/cron/invoice-sync` every 15 minutes to update payment status for users with automatic sync on.

The endpoint `/api/webhooks/dinero` is optional. It marks invoices as paid on `InvoicePaid` events and logs booked and sent events. Requests are verified with an HMAC-SHA256 signature in the `x-dinero-signature` header, using a secret you set on the server and in Dinero:

```dotenv
DINERO_WEBHOOK_SECRET=a-long-random-string
```

TimeWren processes byte-identical signed deliveries once while their record is retained (90 days). A failed delivery can be processed again. This includes payment events received before TimeWren has saved the external invoice ID.
