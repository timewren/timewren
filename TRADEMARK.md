# TimeWren name policy

TimeWren and TimeTrack are names used by Mahope (CVR 39413892) for this project
and for the services Mahope operates. This policy covers the names and logos.
Source licenses do not grant trademark permissions, and this policy does not
restrict the rights to run, modify or redistribute the source that its software
license grants, or lawful referential uses of a name. It does not claim a
registered trademark.

## Welcome uses

Truthful references, compatibility statements, tutorials, reviews and
descriptions of self-hosting are welcome. You may say that your product
integrates with TimeWren or that a fork is based on TimeWren. Do not suggest
that a fork, integration or hosting offer is official or endorsed by Mahope.

## Hosting TimeWren for others

You may host an unmodified TimeWren release for others and call it TimeWren, as
long as you:

- say clearly, on the service and where you sell it, who operates the service;
- do not call the service official or suggest that Mahope operates, endorses or
  supports it.

A modified version that you offer or sell as a service needs its own name and
logo. You may describe it as based on TimeWren.

## Cloud names

"TimeWren Cloud" and "TimeTrack Cloud" are the names of the services Mahope
operates. Do not use them, or confusingly similar names, for any other service,
product, domain or account. Official TimeWren and TimeTrack domains and
accounts are registered to Mahope.

## Forks and modified distributions

For public forks and modified distributions, choose your own product name and
logo. Apart from hosting an unmodified release as described above, seek written
permission from Mahope before using the TimeWren or TimeTrack name or logo as
the name of your own product, service, store listing, domain or merchandise.

## Questions

If you are unsure whether a use is allowed, ask on the project's issue tracker
before you use the name.
