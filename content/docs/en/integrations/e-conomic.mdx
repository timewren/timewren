---
title: "e-conomic"
description: "Connect e-conomic to TimeWren with an app you register yourself: import customers, export clients and invoices, and sync payment status."
---

e-conomic is a Danish accounting system. You register your own app at the e-conomic developer portal and give its two tokens to the server. After that, every user grants the app access to their own e-conomic agreement with one click.

## What it does

- Imports e-conomic customers as clients.
- Exports a client to e-conomic.
- Exports an invoice. You choose layout, payment terms, product, and one line per entry or a combined line. Optionally the invoice is booked on export.
- Syncs payment status back to TimeWren.

## Server setup

1. Create an app at [https://www.e-conomic.com/developer](https://www.e-conomic.com/developer). You get an app public token and an app secret token.
2. Add them to `.env`:

   ```dotenv
   ECONOMIC_APP_PUBLIC_TOKEN=your-public-token
   ECONOMIC_APP_SECRET_TOKEN=your-secret-token
   # Only if the callback differs from NEXT_PUBLIC_APP_URL + /api/integrations/economic/callback
   # ECONOMIC_REDIRECT_URI=https://timewren.example.com/api/integrations/economic/callback
   ```

3. Restart the app.

After a user approves access, e-conomic redirects back to:

```text
https://timewren.example.com/api/integrations/economic/callback
```

The secret token stays on the server and is never shown to users. `ENCRYPTION_KEY` must be set, because each user's agreement grant token is stored encrypted. See [Environment variables](/docs/configuration/environment).

Without both tokens, the Connect button redirects back with an `economic_not_configured` error.

## Connect as a user

1. Open Settings, Integrations and choose Connect to e-conomic.
2. Sign in at e-conomic and approve access for your agreement.
3. Back in TimeWren, choose the default invoice layout, payment terms, products and line settings.
4. Under Default accounting system, optionally turn on Automatic status sync.

Customers are imported from the Sync page under Integrations. Invoices are exported from the invoice itself.

## Payment status

The `cron` container calls `/api/cron/invoice-sync` every 15 minutes to update payment status for users with automatic sync on. You can also run a manual sync from the integrations settings. There is no e-conomic webhook endpoint.
