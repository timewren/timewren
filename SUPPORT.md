# Support

## Community (self-hosting and development)

Free, best effort, in public so answers help the next person.

- **Questions, installation and configuration:** [Discussions, Q&A](https://github.com/timewren/timewren/discussions/categories/q-a).
- **Bugs:** an [issue](https://github.com/timewren/timewren/issues/new/choose) with version, installation type and steps to reproduce.
- **Ideas:** [Discussions, Ideas](https://github.com/timewren/timewren/discussions/categories/ideas).
- **Security problems:** never in public, see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

A maintainer aims to reply to new issues within 3 working days. There is no guaranteed response time for self-hosted installations, and we cannot log in to your server or look at your data.

Useful reading first: the [self-hosting guide](docs/self-hosting.md), the [changelog](CHANGELOG.md) and the [versioning policy](docs/oss/versioning.md) for upgrades.

## TimeTrack Cloud

TimeTrack Cloud is the hosted service run by Mahope on the same code. Customers get support by email at **support@timetrack.dk**: account, billing, data export, imports and anything that needs access to your Cloud data. Please do not post Cloud account details in public issues.
