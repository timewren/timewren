# Governance

## Who decides

TimeWren is led by **Mahope**, the company that created it and runs TimeTrack Cloud. Mahope acts as the project lead ("benevolent dictator for life"): it sets the roadmap, has the final say on scope, design and releases, and appoints maintainers. Today there is one maintainer at Mahope.

Decisions are made in the open where possible: in issues, pull requests and Discussions. Larger changes start as an issue labelled `needs-decision`, and the decision is written down in that issue before work starts.

## Maintainers

Maintainers triage issues, review and merge pull requests and cut releases, following [docs/oss/maintaining.md](docs/oss/maintaining.md). Regular contributors may be invited to become maintainers.

## Commitments

- **No locked features.** Everything is open source under the licenses in [LICENSE](LICENSE). There is no closed enterprise edition, and plans or license keys never switch features on or off. Cloud sells hosting, operations and support.
- **No CLA.** Contributions are accepted under the [Developer Certificate of Origin](CONTRIBUTING.md#developer-certificate-of-origin-dco), and you keep the copyright to your work. Without a CLA, contributed code cannot be relicensed without its authors' consent.
- **Licenses.** Code is AGPL-3.0-only, the browser extension is MIT ([docs/licensing.md](docs/licensing.md)).

## Name and logo

The TimeWren name and logo belong to Mahope and are **not** covered by the AGPL or MIT licenses. Forks must use their own name. See the [trademark policy](TRADEMARK.md).

## Changes to this document

Changes to governance are proposed as a pull request and stay open for at least 14 days before Mahope merges them.
